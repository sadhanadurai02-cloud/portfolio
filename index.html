Section 05

Why I'm betting
on SaaS

Four years of selling taught me that the job is not persuasion — it is diagnosis. The calls that convert are the ones where a real business problem surfaces and a real solution meets it. Software is where that exchange has the highest leverage: the problems are structural, the buyers are measurable, and the value compounds after the signature.

I am drawn to technology because the learning never closes. Modern frameworks — MEDDPICC, discovery-led qualification, multi-channel sequencing — gave shape to instincts I built on the phone, and each new tool sharpens the next hundred conversations.

Predictable revenue is built one qualified conversation at a time, and I want to build it inside a team that measures it that way. First as an SDR who fills the top of the funnel with genuine opportunity. Then as an AE who carries those conversations to close. Long term, as an Enterprise AE selling into the accounts where the diagnosis matters most.
